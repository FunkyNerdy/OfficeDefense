# OfficeDefense
Office Defense Game

Office Defense

Stellar Products, Inc. · Floor 1

A 1960s office tower-defense game. Aliens are crawling out of the air vents. Hire staff, defend the doors, buy more rooms, and keep Floor 1 running.

Play it here: https://FunkyNerdy.github.io/office-defense/

You don't download anything from this page. Open the link above on your phone, add it to your home screen, and it works like an app, even with no internet.

iPhone or iPad
Open the link in Safari. It has to be Safari, not Chrome or the Google app.
Wait for the start menu to appear.
Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
If you don't see it, tap ⋯ first, then Share.
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen," then tap Add.
Open the game once from the new icon while you still have Wi-Fi or data. Leave it on the start menu for about 10 seconds; this is when it saves itself to your phone.

That's it. From now on it opens from the icon and plays offline, in airplane mode too.

Android
Open the link in Chrome.
Tap the ⋮ menu (top right), then Add to Home screen or Install app.
Open it once from the icon while you're online, and leave it on the start menu for about 10 seconds.
On a computer

Just open the link in any browser and play. Use the mouse wheel to zoom, drag to move around the floor, and hold D to see every detail at any zoom.

Good to know
Leaving mid-run: closing the app ends the run you're in. Switching to another app for a moment is usually fine.
Online only:
Playtest notes: the Notes button can't send while you're offline.
My runs: the run history is online only.
Updates arrive by themselves. When there's a new version, open the app twice while online. The first time downloads it; the second shows it.
If something goes wrong
The text looks plain or wrong: you opened it from the icon for the first time while offline. Open it once more with internet.
"404" or a blank page: check the link is typed exactly as above.
The icon won't open the game:
Press and hold the icon, then choose Remove App → Delete from Home Screen.
Do the steps above again.
Still stuck: message whoever sent you the link.
